IP AXIX

User Guide

Your patents, never a missed deadline — a plain-language guide to every feature.

IP Axix keeps every US and European deadline on your patents watched automatically, summarises each office action, forecasts your renewal costs, and lets you ask questions about your portfolio in plain English. This guide walks you through all of it. It applies to all three plans — Individual, Corporate and Practice — with plan differences noted where they matter.

1 · Getting started

  1. Sign in with the email and password you chose when you signed up.
  2. Your Dashboard (Overview) opens — a live picture of your whole portfolio.
  3. If your plan lets you add patents, open Data Manager and paste your patent or application numbers. Everything else builds itself — no data entry, no migration.

2 · What each tab does

🏠
Overview (Dashboard)Your portfolio at a glance — health score, granted vs. pending, jurisdictions (US & European), upcoming deadlines, an A–F strength grade for each patent, and who's citing your patents.
📁
PatentsEvery patent you hold. Click one to open its full record — status, claims, patent family, documents and its complete prosecution timeline.
📡
Data ManagerAdd patents by number and pull them live from the USPTO & EPO — no data entry. (Available to portfolio owners and admins.)
📜
ProsecutionThe step-by-step history of a case — office actions, responses, fees — with each office action summarised for you by AI the day it lands.
📊
Activity ReportEvery prosecution event across your whole portfolio for a period you choose.
🧠
IntelligenceCompetitor watch — which companies cite your patents (US & European) — your licensing and enforcement shortlist.
🤖
AI AssistantAsk anything about your portfolio in plain English — “what's due next quarter?”, “which cases got a final rejection?”, “what will renewals cost me?”
🗂
ActionsThe shared to-do list between attorney and client. Accept a deadline and it becomes an action both sides track in real time.
✨
PlansThe three plans — Individual, Corporate, Practice — and exactly what each includes.

3 · Everyday tasks, step by step

→ Add a patentData Manager → paste the patent or application number → “Add to Portfolio”. It syncs from the offices automatically and your board updates.
→ See what's dueThe Overview shows upcoming deadlines; every date is watched and you're reminded well before it falls due.
→ Understand an office actionOpen the patent → Prosecution → click the event → read the plain-language AI summary (what's rejected, what's asked, the response date).
→ Know your renewal costsYour dashboard/report shows the year-by-year forecast — US maintenance fees and European renewals — so there are no surprise bills.
→ Ask the AIAI Assistant → type your question → get the answer straight from your own data.
→ Invite an attorney (Corporate)Groups & Attorneys → invite by email → allocate the patents they should handle. Each attorney sees only their allocated cases.
→ Invite a client (Practice)Groups & Clients → invite by email. Each client gets their own secure login and sees only their own patents.
→ Accept a deadline as an actionOn a deadline, choose Accept — it appears in Actions for your attorney/client to track in real time.
→ Get helpUse the “Queries / Contact” button in the bottom corner, or email IPaxis@proton.me.

4 · What's in your plan

Individual — one owner, one portfolio

Full access to manage your own patents and use every feature above. Inviting attorneys or clients isn't part of this plan. The free demo covers up to 3 patents for 30 days.

Corporate — one in-house team, many attorneys

You see the whole portfolio; invite attorneys and allocate patents so each sees only theirs; work moves between attorney and client in real time. Priced by portfolio size (up to 500 · 1,000 · 3,000+ patents).

Practice — one firm, many clients

Each client gets their own secure login seeing only their patents; you run everything from one dashboard. Priced by number of clients (up to 10 · 50 · 100+).

⚠️ Some tabs or features may be hidden or view-only depending on your plan and what your administrator has enabled. If you expect something you don't see, just contact us.
