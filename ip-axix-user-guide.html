IP AXIX

User Guide

Your patents, never a missed deadline — a plain-language guide to every feature.

IP Axix keeps every US and European deadline on your patents watched automatically, summarises each office action, forecasts your renewal costs, and lets you ask questions about your portfolio in plain English. This guide walks you through all of it. It applies to all three plans — Individual, Corporate and Practice — with plan differences noted where they matter.

1 · Getting started

  1. Sign in with the email and password you chose when you signed up.
  2. Your Dashboard (Overview) opens — a live picture of your whole portfolio.
  3. If your plan lets you add patents, open Data Manager and paste your patent or application numbers. Everything else builds itself — no data entry, no migration.

3 · Adding your patents & how your data fills in

  1. Open 📡 Data Manager.
  2. Type or paste your patent or application numbers — one per line to add several at once (US and European are both fine).
  3. Click “Add to Portfolio”. The patents appear immediately in your Patents list and on the Overview.
  4. IP Axix then pulls the full prosecution history live from the USPTO and EPO in the background. You'll see events, deadlines, office actions and documents begin to appear in the 📊 Activity Report and Prosecution tabs.
  5. As dates arrive, reminders switch on automatically — nothing else to set up.
⏳ This population takes a little time. Depending on how many patents you added (and the patent offices' own response speed), the prosecution data can take from a few minutes to a few hours to finish loading. You don't need to wait or keep the page open — check back on the Activity Report and the data will be there. Larger portfolios simply take longer to complete.

4 · From office action to done — the workflow

  1. See the office action. Open 📊 Activity Report → the Activity Log lists every prosecution event exactly as the patent office reported it — including each office action (OA), the day it posts.
  2. Find the deadline it creates. At the top of the Activity Report, open the “⏳ Docket” sub-tab. Every deadline is calculated for you — the OA response date, plus issue fees, US maintenance fees and European renewals.
  3. Approve it into Actions. On a deadline, click “Docket” to approve it. It's created as an action and moves to the 🗂 Actions tab — and it becomes visible to the client straight away.
  4. The attorney works it. In Actions, the attorney (or your team) moves the item through its status: Pending → Processing → Completed. The client watches the status change in real time — no status-request emails.
  5. Two-way communication. Both sides add comments and attach documents right on the action item — questions, drafts and confirmations live on the deadline itself, so nothing gets lost in email.

5 · Everyday tasks, step by step

→ See what's dueThe Overview shows upcoming deadlines at a glance; every date — maintenance fees, renewals, office-action responses — is watched and you're reminded well before it falls due.
→ Understand an office actionOpen the patent → Prosecution → click the office-action event → read the plain-language AI summary: what's being rejected, what's being asked, and the exact response deadline. The original document is one click away.
→ Know your renewal costsYour dashboard and report show the year-by-year forecast — US maintenance fees and European renewals across the whole portfolio — so renewal season is a decision, never a surprise bill.
→ Ask the AI AssistantOpen 🤖 AI Assistant and type a question in plain English — “what's due next quarter?”, “which cases got a final rejection?”, “what's my renewal spend in 2027?” — and get the answer straight from your own live data.
→ Invite an attorney & allocate patents (Corporate)Groups & Attorneys → invite the attorney by email → choose which patents they handle. They get their own login and see only their allocated cases; you keep the whole-portfolio view.
→ Invite a client to their own portal (Practice)Groups & Clients → invite the client by email. They get their own secure login showing only their patents and deadlines, while you run every client from one dashboard.
→ Accept a deadline as an actionOn any deadline, choose Accept — it becomes an item in 🗂 Actions that you and your attorney/client both track in real time, with comments and documents.
→ Get help or send a queryUse the “Queries / Contact” button in the bottom corner of any screen, or email IPaxis@proton.me. We usually reply the same day.

6 · What's in your plan

Individual — one owner, one portfolio

Full access to manage your own patents and use every feature above. Inviting attorneys or clients isn't part of this plan. The free demo covers up to 3 patents for 30 days.

Corporate — one in-house team, many attorneys

You see the whole portfolio; invite attorneys and allocate patents so each sees only theirs; work moves between attorney and client in real time. Priced by portfolio size (up to 500 · 1,000 · 3,000+ patents).

Practice — one firm, many clients

Each client gets their own secure login seeing only their patents; you run everything from one dashboard. Priced by number of clients (up to 10 · 50 · 100+).

⚠️ Some tabs or features may be hidden or view-only depending on your plan and what your administrator has enabled. If you expect something you don't see, just contact us.
