IP AXIX

User Guide

Your patents, never a missed deadline — a plain-language guide to every feature.

IP Axix keeps every US and European deadline on your patents watched automatically, summarises each office action, forecasts your renewal costs, and lets you ask questions about your portfolio in plain English. This guide walks you through all of it. It applies to all three plans — Individual, Corporate and Practice — with plan differences noted where they matter.

1 · Getting started

  1. Sign in with the email and password you chose when you signed up.
  2. Your Dashboard (Overview) opens — a live picture of your whole portfolio.
  3. If your plan lets you add patents, open Data Manager and paste your patent or application numbers. Everything else builds itself — no data entry, no migration.

3 · Step-by-step workflows — everything you can do

1 · Add patents & get your data

  1. Open 📡 Data Manager, type or paste your patent / application numbers (one per line for several — US and European both fine), and click “Add to Portfolio”. They appear immediately in Patents and on the Overview.
  2. IP Axix pulls the full prosecution history live from the USPTO & EPO in the background — events, deadlines, office actions and documents appear in the 📊 Activity Report and Prosecution tabs. Reminders switch on automatically.
⏳ Data population takes a little time — from a few minutes to a few hours depending on how many patents you added (and the offices' own response speed). You don't need to wait or keep the page open; check back on the Activity Report and it'll be there. Larger portfolios take longer.

2 · Read your dashboard (Overview)

  • Portfolio health — a 0–100 score for the overall shape of your portfolio.
  • Composition — granted vs. pending, jurisdictions (US & European) and technology categories; click a slice to list those patents.
  • Upcoming deadlines — what's due soon across the whole portfolio.
  • Patent strength grade — an A–F grade per patent from forward citations plus remaining term (US & European).
  • Competitor watch — which companies cite your patents.

3 · Explore a single patent (Patents)

  • Open the record — click any patent to see its status, claims (independent & dependent), patent family, inventors, assignee and key dates.
  • Documents — open the filed documents attached to the case.
  • Timeline — view the patent's full prosecution timeline in one place.

4 · Prosecution & office actions

  • The Activity Log — 📊 Activity Report → Activity Log lists every prosecution event exactly as the office reported it, including each office action (OA).
  • AI summaries — click an office action to read the plain-language summary: what's rejected, what's asked, the response deadline.
  • Original document — open the underlying office document in one click.

5 · Deadlines & the docket

  • The Docket — at the top of the Activity Report, open the “⏳ Docket” sub-tab; every deadline is calculated for you: OA responses, issue fees, US maintenance fees and European renewals.
  • Filter — to what's already open in Actions, or what's completed.
  • Reminders — you're reminded well before each date.

6 · From office action to done (the Actions workflow)

  1. Approve it. On a deadline, click “Docket” → it becomes an action in the 🗂 Actions tab, visible to the client straight away.
  2. Set the status. The attorney/team moves the item Pending → Processing → Completed; the client watches it change in real time — no status emails.
  3. Raise a request. Create a request or question as its own action item when you need something from the other side.
  4. Two-way communication. Both sides add comments and attach documents right on the item — nothing gets lost in email.
  5. Track completion. Completed items stay visible; filter Actions to see exactly what's done.

7 · Your team & who sees what

  • Corporate — Groups & Attorneys → invite an attorney → allocate their patents. Each attorney sees only their allocated cases and moves them Pending → Processing → Completed (proceed / do-not-proceed); you keep the whole-portfolio view.
  • Practice — Groups & Clients → invite a client. Each client gets their own secure login showing only their patents; you run every client from one dashboard.
  • Individual — one owner, full access; inviting others isn't part of this plan.

8 · Costs, reminders & reports

  • Renewal-cost forecast — year by year, US maintenance fees and European renewals.
  • Send a reminder — email an upcoming-deadline reminder to whoever needs it.
  • Status report — generate and email a report; choose which statuses to include (Completed / Processing / Pending).
  • Export — export your lists to CSV.

9 · Ask the AI Assistant

  • Open 🤖 AI Assistant and type in plain English — “what's due next quarter?”, “which cases got a final rejection?”, “what's my renewal spend in 2027?” — and get the answer straight from your own live data.

10 · Intelligence & competition

  • Competitor watch — 🧠 Intelligence shows which companies cite your patents (US & European).
  • Drill in — open a company to see exactly which of your patents they cite.

11 · Keeping your data current

  • Automatic refresh — your portfolio refreshes twice a week; new events and deadlines appear on their own.
  • Full Sync — (owners/admins) run a Full Sync from the Activity Report to pull the latest immediately.

4 · What's in your plan

Startup / Small firm — for startups, small teams & solo inventors

Full access to manage your own patents and use every feature above — up to 25 patents and 2 users. Inviting attorneys or clients isn't part of this plan. The free demo covers up to 3 patents for 30 days.

Corporate — companies managing their own portfolio

You see the whole portfolio; invite attorneys and allocate patents so each sees only theirs; work moves between attorney and client in real time. Priced by portfolio size (up to 3,000+ patents, unlimited seats).

Practice — law firms & agents managing many clients

Each client gets their own secure login seeing only their patents; you run everything from one dashboard. Priced by number of client portfolios, white-label available.

Enterprise — large corporates with security & scale needs

Everything in Practice, plus SSO and DPA review, an uptime SLA and dedicated success, custom limits and integrations, and data-residency options.

⚠️ Some tabs or features may be hidden or view-only depending on your plan and what your administrator has enabled. If you expect something you don't see, just contact us.
