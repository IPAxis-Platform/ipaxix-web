Good to know

What counts as a "patent"?

Each patent or application you track in your portfolio counts as one — across its life, from pending application through grant and renewals. You're billed on the band your portfolio falls into, not on exact counts, so there's headroom to grow within a band before the next one.

Is it really unlimited users?

Yes. On Corporate and Practice there's no per-seat fee — invite as many attorneys or team members as you need. Access is controlled by allocation, so each person sees only the cases that are theirs.

How is billing handled?

Prices are annual and in USD, excluding local taxes. Monthly billing is available on request. Multi-year terms carry a discount — ask us. You can start on the free 30-day trial (up to 3 patents, no card) and move to a paid plan whenever you're ready; your data carries over untouched.

What about India (IN) patents?

Deadlines for Indian patents are supported and calculated in your tracker. Because Indian patents aren't in the public lookup databases, they're added by number manually rather than pulled automatically — everything after that works the same.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime. Moving between bands or plans is instant and prorated — your patents, history and deadlines stay exactly as they are.
