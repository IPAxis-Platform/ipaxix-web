Questions, answered

How is IP Axix priced?▾

By the size of your portfolio (number of patents), not per user — so your whole team, your attorneys and your clients can all work in the tool. You only move up a band when your portfolio grows. Book a demo and we'll quote your exact portfolio.

Which jurisdictions are covered?▾

The United States (USPTO), Europe (EPO plus national validations and Unitary Patents) and India — with prosecution history, deadlines and documents. Other jurisdictions can be tracked by date.

Is my data secure?▾

Yes. Each client's data is isolated at the database level with row-level security, encrypted in transit and at rest. A Data Processing Agreement is available for Corporate plans and above.

Do you offer a trial?▾

Start free — monitor up to 3 patents for 30 days, no card required. The full platform is then set up with you personally.

What if my portfolio grows past a band?▾

Move up a band at any time; we prorate the difference. You're never cut off mid-cycle — deadlines keep being watched.

Can partners resell IP Axix?▾

Yes — IP service firms, docketing bureaus and patent practices can offer IP Axix to their clients on partner terms, including white-label for the Practice tier. Book a call to discuss.
